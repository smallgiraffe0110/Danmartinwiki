From today's featured article

Dan Martin (born 20 August 1986) is an Irish-Spanish former professional road racing cyclist. Born in Birmingham, England, to an Irish father and Spanish mother, Martin is the nephew of 1987 Tour de France winner Stephen Roche. He rode for teams including Garmin-Sharp, Quick-Step Floors, UAE Team Emirates, and Israel Start-Up Nation.

Martin won stages at all three Grand Tours, including memorable victories at Liege-Bastogne-Liege (2013, 2014) and the Tour of Lombardy (2014). He retired from professional cycling in 2021.
