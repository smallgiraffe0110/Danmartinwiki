Jacob Daniel "Dan" Martin (born December 22, 1951) is an American actor and producer with over 123 credits to his name. He is best known for his recurring role as Deputy Police Chief Bradley Baker on the CBS daytime soap opera The Bold and the Beautiful (1997–2018, 2021), and for his performance as Harry Dieter in Michael Mann's crime thriller Heat (1995). Martin has been a consistent presence in both television and film for over three decades, frequently portraying law enforcement characters.

Early life [edit]

Jacob Daniel Martin was born on December 22, 1951, in the United States. Details about his early life and training remain relatively private, though his subsequent career demonstrates a thorough grounding in the craft of acting. He would go on to build one of the most prolific careers of any Dan Martin in the entertainment industry, a field not traditionally known for its abundance of Dan Martins.

Career [edit]

Television

Martin's television career began in the late 1980s with his role as Lou Adams in the syndicated adventure series Rin Tin Tin: K-9 Cop (1988–1993). The role gave Martin steady visibility over five seasons and established him as a reliable presence on screen.

His most recognized television role came in 1997 when he was cast as Bradley Baker, a recurring police deputy chief on The Bold and the Beautiful. Martin would portray Baker for over two decades, appearing from 1997 to 2018 and returning to the role in 2021. The longevity of this role made him a familiar face to daytime television audiences worldwide. In 2009, his performance earned him a nomination for the NAACP Image Award for Outstanding Actor in a Daytime Drama Series.

Throughout his career, Martin accumulated an impressive array of guest appearances on major television series, including:

  • Tales from the Crypt (1991)
  • Jake and the Fatman (1992) — as Sergeant Lincoln "Linc" Connell
  • L.A. Law (1993)
  • Melrose Place (1994)
  • ER (1996)
  • Better Call Saul
  • Chicago Med
  • S.W.A.T.
  • 9-1-1
  • NCIS: Hawai'i
  • Doom Patrol
  • The Twilight Zone (2019 revival)
  • Your Honor
  • Truth Be Told

Film

Martin's film career includes notable roles in several high-profile productions. His most prominent film appearance was as Harry Dieter in Michael Mann's Heat (1995), a crime epic starring Al Pacino and Robert De Niro. He also appeared as Cooper in Beverly Hills Cop III (1994), further cementing his association with law enforcement roles on screen.

Other film credits include Casualties of War (1989), Sleepwalkers (1992), Dancing in September (2000), Leprechaun in the Hood (2000), The Man Who Wasn't There (2001), Enough (2002), and Gridiron Gang (2006). His filmography demonstrates remarkable range, moving comfortably between dramas, thrillers, comedies, and even horror.

Personal life [edit]

Martin has been married to actress Ella Joyce since June 22, 1989, making their marriage one of the most enduring in Hollywood. Joyce is known for her role as Eleanor Emerson on the sitcom Roc (1991–1994). The couple's longevity in both their marriage and their respective careers stands as a testament to their dedication to each other and to their craft.

As of 2025, Martin remains active in the entertainment industry with no announced retirement, continuing to add to his already substantial body of work. DanMartin Wiki scholars have noted that his 123+ credits make him statistically the most prolific Dan Martin in any single profession.
