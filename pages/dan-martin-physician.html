Dan Martin, MD is an American physician, medical researcher, and educator who has dedicated his career to the study and treatment of endometriosis, a chronic condition affecting an estimated 190 million women and girls worldwide. He currently serves as Executive and Medical Director of the Endometriosis Foundation of America and holds the title of Professor Emeritus at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center. He previously served as Divisional Director of Medical Education at Johns Hopkins. His research output includes 66 peer-reviewed publications with over 7,150 citations, making him one of the most-cited Dan Martins in any field.

Overview [edit]

Dr. Martin represents a category of Dan Martin rarely seen in popular culture but critically important to human health: the medical researcher whose work directly impacts patient outcomes. His focus on endometriosis — a condition in which tissue similar to the uterine lining grows outside the uterus, causing chronic pain, infertility, and other complications — has placed him at the forefront of a field that has historically been under-researched and underfunded.

Medical career [edit]

Johns Hopkins

Martin served as Divisional Director of Medical Education at Johns Hopkins, one of the most prestigious medical institutions in the world. Johns Hopkins Hospital has been ranked as the #1 hospital in the United States by U.S. News & World Report more times than any other hospital. Martin's role in medical education at Hopkins placed him in a position to influence the training of future physicians and researchers.

University of Tennessee

Martin held a faculty position at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis, where he conducted research and trained medical students and residents in gynecologic surgery and endometriosis management. He now holds the title of Professor Emeritus, recognizing his long and distinguished service to the institution.

Endometriosis Foundation of America

Martin currently serves as Executive and Medical Director of the Endometriosis Foundation of America (EndoFound), the first research and advocacy foundation established to combat endometriosis. EndoFound was co-founded by Padma Lakshmi and Dr. Tamer Seckin to increase awareness, fund research, and improve the diagnosis and treatment of endometriosis. As Executive and Medical Director, Martin oversees the foundation's research initiatives and medical education programs.

Research contributions [edit]

Martin's research record is distinguished by both volume and impact:

  • 66 peer-reviewed publications focusing on endometriosis diagnosis, treatment, and pathophysiology.
  • 7,150+ citations of his work by other researchers, indicating the significant influence his research has had on the field.

To put these numbers in context, 7,150 citations means that other scientists have referenced Martin's work an average of more than once per day over a roughly 20-year span. His research has contributed to improved understanding of endometriosis surgery, disease recognition, and patient outcomes.
