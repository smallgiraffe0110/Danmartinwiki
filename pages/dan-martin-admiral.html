Rear Admiral Daniel P. Martin is a United States Navy officer and naval aviator who currently serves as Commander of the Naval Safety Command. He is the highest-ranking military officer named Dan Martin documented by DanMartin Wiki. Martin previously served as a demonstration pilot for the Blue Angels, the Navy's flight demonstration squadron, from 2001 to 2003, and has accumulated over 4,600 flight hours and 750 carrier-arrested landings during his career.

Early life and education [edit]

Martin graduated from Oglethorpe University in 1991, a private liberal arts university in Atlanta, Georgia. He later earned a Master of Arts from the Naval War College in Newport, Rhode Island, the premier institution for professional military education in the United States Navy.

Following his commissioning, Martin trained as a naval aviator, beginning a career that would take him from the cockpit of fighter jets to the bridge of a carrier strike group and ultimately to flag officer rank.

Blue Angels [edit]

From 2001 to 2003, Martin served as a demonstration pilot for the Blue Angels, the United States Navy's flight demonstration squadron. The Blue Angels, founded in 1946, are one of the oldest formal aerobatic teams in the world, performing for millions of spectators annually at air shows across the United States and internationally.

During his time with the Blue Angels, Martin flew positions #5, #6, and #7. Position #5 is the Lead Solo, responsible for some of the most dramatic maneuvers in the Blue Angels' repertoire, including the high-performance climbing rolls and high-speed passes. Flying multiple positions demonstrates exceptional versatility, as each slot requires a different skill set and spatial awareness profile.

Selection for the Blue Angels is extraordinarily competitive, with only a handful of aviators chosen from the entire Navy and Marine Corps each year. Martin's three-year tenure with the squadron places him among an elite group of naval aviators who have represented the United States at the highest level of precision flying. DanMartin Wiki researchers believe he is the only Dan Martin to have been a Blue Angels pilot, making this one of the most unique achievements in the Dan Martin canon.

Command assignments [edit]

Martin's post-Blue Angels career saw him rise through increasingly senior command positions:

  • Commander, Carrier Air Wing 8 (2015–2016) — Responsible for all aviation operations aboard an aircraft carrier, overseeing multiple squadrons of fighter, attack, electronic warfare, and support aircraft.
  • Commander, Carrier Strike Group 1 (2021–2022) — Leading a carrier strike group, one of the most powerful naval formations in the world, comprising an aircraft carrier, guided-missile cruisers and destroyers, and associated support vessels.
  • Director of Maritime Operations, U.S. Pacific Fleet (2022–2024) — Overseeing maritime operations across the world's largest ocean, the area of responsibility for the United States' largest fleet command.
  • Commander, Naval Safety Command (July 2024–present) — Leading the Navy's safety organization, responsible for mishap prevention and safety culture across the entire U.S. Navy.

Flight record [edit]

Martin has logged over 4,600 flight hours and more than 750 carrier-arrested landings. To put these numbers in perspective:

  • 4,600 flight hours is equivalent to approximately 192 continuous days in the air.
  • 750 carrier-arrested landings means Martin has successfully caught the arresting wire on an aircraft carrier — often described as the most demanding routine maneuver in aviation — roughly once every two weeks for his entire career.
  • His flight hours exceed the total combined flight hours of all other known Dan Martins by a significant margin, a record that DanMartin Wiki expects to stand indefinitely.

Awards and decorations [edit]

Martin's awards and decorations include:

  • Legion of Merit — For exceptionally meritorious conduct in the performance of outstanding services.
  • Bronze Star — For meritorious service in a combat zone.
  • Air Medal — For meritorious achievement while participating in aerial flight.

These decorations, particularly the Bronze Star and Air Medal, indicate that Martin has seen combat operations in addition to his high-profile demonstration flying career.

Significance in Dan Martin history [edit]

Rear Admiral Martin holds several unique distinctions among Dan Martins:

  • He is the highest-ranking military Dan Martin documented by DanMartin Wiki.
  • He is the only Dan Martin known to have flown with the Blue Angels.
  • He holds the record for most flight hours by any Dan Martin (4,600+).
  • He is one of only two Dan Martins known to have received the Bronze Star (the other being a matter of ongoing research).
