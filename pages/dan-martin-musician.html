Dan Martin is a name shared by several musicians across various genres, time periods, and geographies. This article serves as a compilation of notable musicians who bear this remarkably common yet distinguished name.

Overview [edit]

The name Dan Martin has appeared across the musical landscape with surprising frequency. From singer-songwriters to session musicians, from indie rock performers to classical artists, the name has been associated with musical talent across genres. This page documents the known Dan Martins who have made their mark in the world of music.

Notable musicians named Dan Martin [edit]

Dan Martin (singer-songwriter)

Various singer-songwriters named Dan Martin have emerged in the indie and folk music scenes. Dan Martin (session musician)

As a result, cataloging all Dan Martins who have worked as session musicians is a task that DanMartin Wiki scholars continue to pursue.

Dan Martin (band member)

Multiple bands across rock, punk, jazz, and other genres have counted a Dan Martin among their members. The name's prevalence ensures that at any given time, there is likely at least one active band somewhere in the world featuring a Dan Martin.

The name "Dan Martin" in music [edit]

Beyond musicians who bear the name, "Dan Martin" has occasionally appeared as a character name in songs, a pseudonym for recording artists, and even as an album or track title. The name's combination of simplicity and universality gives it a certain musical quality that songwriters have recognized.

Music historians at DanMartin Wiki have noted that the two-syllable, two-name combination has a rhythmic quality that lends itself well to lyrics, much like other common names that have become fixtures in popular music.
