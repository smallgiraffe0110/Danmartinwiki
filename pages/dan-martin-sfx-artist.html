Dan Martin is a British practical effects and special makeup effects artist, widely regarded as one of the most in-demand practitioners in horror and genre cinema today. He is known for his work on The Banshees of Inisherin (2022), Brandon Cronenberg's Possessor (2020) and Infinity Pool (2023), Ben Wheatley's High-Rise (2015), and numerous other films in the horror, science fiction, and thriller genres. Martin specializes in practical, in-camera effects — prosthetics, creature work, and gore — at a time when much of the film industry has shifted to digital alternatives.

Overview [edit]

In an era dominated by computer-generated imagery, Martin has built his reputation as a champion of practical effects — physical, tangible creations that exist on set and are photographed by the camera rather than added in post-production. His work spans prosthetic makeup, animatronics, creature design, and gore effects, all executed with a craftsmanship that has earned him a loyal following among directors who prefer the tactile reality of practical work.

DanMartin Wiki notes that Martin is one of two Dan Martins prominently associated with the film industry, alongside Dan Martin (actor). However, while the actor Dan Martin appears in front of the camera, this Dan Martin's work appears on screen in a far more visceral fashion — often literally under the skin of the characters.

Career [edit]

Early work

Martin established himself in the British independent film scene, working on genre productions that demanded creative, low-budget practical effects solutions. His early credits include The Human Centipede II (Full Sequence) (2011), a project that required extensive prosthetic work and demonstrated Martin's ability to realize extreme concepts with practical methods. He also worked on Lords of Chaos (2018), a film about the Norwegian black metal scene that required period-accurate makeup and effects.

Brandon Cronenberg collaborations

Martin developed a particularly notable creative partnership with director Brandon Cronenberg, son of body horror pioneer David Cronenberg. Martin provided the practical effects for Cronenberg's Possessor (2020), a science fiction thriller that required sophisticated body transformation and violence effects, and Infinity Pool (2023), which demanded equally challenging work. These collaborations cemented Martin's reputation as an artist capable of realizing the most demanding visions in contemporary body horror.

The Cronenberg connection is fitting, as Brandon's father David Cronenberg is one of the filmmakers most associated with practical effects in horror cinema. Martin's work with Brandon represents a generational continuation of the practical effects tradition in body horror filmmaking.

Mainstream and award-nominated films

Martin's most high-profile credit came with The Banshees of Inisherin (2022), directed by Martin McDonagh. The film, starring Colin Farrell and Brendan Gleeson, was nominated for nine Academy Awards including Best Picture. Martin served as prosthetic effects designer, creating effects that were integral to the film's plot. This credit placed a Dan Martin's work at the center of one of the most acclaimed films of its year.

He also provided effects for Ben Wheatley's High-Rise (2015), an adaptation of J.G. Ballard's dystopian novel, and Azrael (2024), for which he served as creature effects designer.

Approach to practical effects [edit]

Martin is a vocal advocate for practical effects in an industry increasingly dominated by CGI. In interviews, he has emphasized the importance of giving actors something real to interact with on set, arguing that practical effects create performances and reactions that are impossible to replicate when actors are working against green screens and tennis balls on sticks.

His workshop produces custom prosthetics, animatronic creatures, and mechanical effects rigs tailored to each production's specific needs. This bespoke approach ensures that every effect is designed for its narrative context rather than applied as a generic solution.
